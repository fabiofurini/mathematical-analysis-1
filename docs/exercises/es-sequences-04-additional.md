!!! esercizio "Exercise 1"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{2^{-n} + 3\: n^3}{\log^6 n +2 - n^5}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 2"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{\log n + 3\: n^3 \: \log n}{2^{1/n} + n^5}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 3"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{n^2 + \log^3 n}{n \: \log n +1 }
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 4"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{n^{2+ \frac{1}{n}}}{1 + n^{3-\frac{2}{n}}}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 5"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \left( \frac{n}{n+1}\right)^{2 \:n}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 6"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \left( \frac{n+2}{n+1}\right)^{n^2}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 7"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \left( 1 + \frac{3}{n}\right)^{-n}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 8"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \left( 1 - \frac{2}{n}\right)^{n}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 9"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip}  \frac{n + \log (n^2) - 2^n}{(\log n)^3 + n^2}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 10"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip}  \frac{8 + 2\:n -n ^{4/3}}{n^{-1/3} + 3\:n - n ^{5/3}}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 11"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} n \left( \sqrt{n^2+3} - n \right)
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 12"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{n^2 + \log(5\: n^3) + (\log n)^2}{2\: (\log n)^3 + n\: \log n}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 13"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{8 + 2\: n^2 - n^{4/3}}{n^{-1/3} + 3\: n^2 - n^{5/3}}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 14"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \left( \sqrt{n^4+1} - n\: \sqrt{n^2+1} \right)
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 15"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{n^2 + 3^n + 2^{-n}}{2^{2\:n} +(\log n)^3}
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 16"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{1}{n} \: (\sin^2 n) \cdot \sin (n^2)
    $$

!!! esercizio "Exercise 17"

    Compute, if it exists, the limit of the sequence, justifying the steps taken:

    $$
    \lim_{n \rr \ip} \frac{\cos (\pi \: n)}{\pi \: n} + \log \left( \sin \frac{\pi}{n}\right)
    $$
