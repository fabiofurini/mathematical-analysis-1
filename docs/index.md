Teaching material conceived and developed by **[Fabio Furini](https://sites.google.com/view/fabiofurini/home-page)**, associate
professor at [DIAG](https://www.diag.uniroma1.it/), Sapienza University of Rome.

**The course lecture notes, online**: definitions, theorems with their
proofs, examples worked out step by step, exercises and **interactive graphs**
to watch mathematics move.

## How each chapter is organized

Each chapter is one of the course notes, with the same numbers and the same
colors: on the website and in the PDF you find everything in the same place.

<div class="grid" markdown>

!!! definizione "Definition"
    The precise meaning of a new concept.

!!! teorema "Theorem"
    A result to remember, with its hypotheses.

!!! esempio "Example"
    A computation worked out in full, one step at a time.

!!! chiave ""
    **In green** the key point to take away.

</div>

??? dimostrazione "Proof — opens with a click"
    All proofs are included, but folded: first read the statement, then open
    the proof when you want to study it.

## The parts of the course

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-numeric: **1 · Numbers and logic**

    ---

    Sets, logic and proof techniques, real numbers and supremum, summations,
    induction, binomial coefficients, complex numbers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](numbers/index.md)

-   :material-function-variant: **2 · Functions**

    ---

    Elementary functions and their graphs: powers, exponentials and
    logarithms, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, composite and inverse functions.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](functions/index.md)

-   :material-dots-horizontal: **3 · Limits of sequences**

    ---

    Sequences and limits, Euler's number, comparisons and asymptotic
    estimates, hierarchies of infinities, recursive sequences.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](sequences/index.md)

-   :material-chart-bell-curve: **3 · Limits of functions and continuity**

    ---

    Limits and asymptotes, standard limits, continuous functions, zeros
    theorem and bisection, Weierstrass and intermediate values.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](limits/index.md)

-   :material-chart-line: **4 · Derivatives**

    ---

    The derivative and the rules of differentiation, Fermat and Lagrange,
    L'Hôpital, second derivative, Taylor, curve sketching, Newton's method.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](derivatives/index.md)

-   :material-sigma: **5 · Series**

    ---

    Numerical series, convergence tests for series with non-negative terms,
    series with terms of varying sign, series of functions.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](series/index.md)

</div>
