## The parts of the course

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-lightbulb-on-outline: **1 · Logic and proof methods**

    ---

    Logical symbols and implications, the contrapositive, necessary and
    sufficient conditions, proofs by contradiction, mathematical induction.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](logic/index.md)

-   :material-numeric: **2 · Numbers and sets**

    ---

    Sets, real numbers and supremum, roots and logarithms, summations,
    binomial coefficients, infinite sets, complex numbers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](numbers/index.md)

-   :material-relation-many-to-many: **3 · Binary relations**

    ---

    Properties of relations, directed graphs, equivalence and order
    relations: functions are special relations.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapter](relations/index.md)

-   :material-function-variant: **3 · Functions**

    ---

    Elementary functions and their graphs: powers, exponentials and
    logarithms, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, composite and inverse functions.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](functions/index.md)

-   :material-dots-horizontal: **4 · Limits of sequences**

    ---

    Sequences and limits, Euler's number, comparisons and asymptotic
    estimates, hierarchies of infinities, recursive sequences.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](sequences/index.md)

-   :material-chart-bell-curve: **4 · Limits of functions and continuity**

    ---

    Limits and asymptotes, standard limits, continuous functions, zeros
    theorem and bisection, Weierstrass and intermediate values.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](limits/index.md)

-   :material-chart-line: **5 · Derivatives**

    ---

    The derivative and the rules of differentiation, Fermat and Lagrange,
    L'Hôpital, second derivative, Taylor, curve sketching, Newton's method.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](derivatives/index.md)

-   :material-sigma: **6 · Series**

    ---

    Numerical series, convergence tests for series with non-negative terms,
    series with terms of varying sign, series of functions.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](series/index.md)

</div>

## The series

Ten courses built the same way: lecture notes online and in PDF, exercises with solutions, an interactive lab and self-assessment quizzes.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-function-variant: **Mathematical Analysis 1** · *this course*

    real numbers, functions, limits, derivatives and series, with interactive graphs

-   :material-matrix: **[Linear Algebra](https://fabiofurini.github.io/linear-algebra/)**

    vectors, matrices, determinants, linear systems and eigenvalues, with the computation lab

-   :material-code-braces: **[Algorithms](https://fabiofurini.github.io/algorithms/)**

    number representation, pseudo-code, sorting and searching, analysis and recursive algorithms

-   :material-vector-triangle: **[Linear Programming](https://fabiofurini.github.io/linear-programming/)**

    polyhedra, the simplex algorithm, duality and the dual simplex, with the interactive lab

-   :material-dots-grid: **[Integer Linear Programming](https://fabiofurini.github.io/integer-linear-programming/)**

    LP relaxation, valid inequalities, cutting planes, branch-and-bound and branch-and-cut

-   :material-vector-curve: **[Convex Optimization](https://fabiofurini.github.io/convex-optimization/)**

    convex sets and functions, convex optimization problems, with interactive graphs

-   :material-graph-outline: **[Network Optimization](https://fabiofurini.github.io/network-optimization/)**

    graphs, spanning trees, shortest paths, network flows, the network simplex and project management

-   :material-hexagon-multiple-outline: **[Combinatorial Optimization](https://fabiofurini.github.io/combinatorial-optimization/)**

    knapsack, bin packing, coloring, TSP, scheduling and lot sizing, solved in every way

-   :material-vector-polygon: **[MIP Modelling](https://fabiofurini.github.io/mip-modelling/)**

    integer linear programming models, from the formulation to the solution with solvers

-   :material-chart-timeline-variant: **[Operations Research Lab](https://fabiofurini.github.io/operations-research-lab/)**

    modelling and solving optimization problems with Python and solvers

</div>
