# The course

## How to use this website

The website contains the **lecture notes** of the Mathematical Analysis 1
course, divided into five parts as in the PDF: numbers and logic, functions,
limits (of sequences and of functions), derivatives, series. Each chapter can
be read from beginning to end, or you can jump directly to a section from the
menu on the right.

- **The colored boxes** have the same color and number as in the PDF:
  <span style="color:var(--c-definizione)">**definitions**</span>,
  <span style="color:var(--c-teorema)">**theorems**</span>,
  <span style="color:var(--c-esempio)">**worked examples**</span>,
  <span style="color:var(--c-chiave)">**key points**</span>.
- **Proofs** are folded: they open with a click.
- **The PDF of each chapter** can be downloaded from the link at the top of the page.
- **Search** (at the top) finds words and formulas across all the notes.
- **Light or dark theme**: with the icon at the top right.
- On a phone the website reads well: long formulas scroll horizontally.

## How to study

1. Read the chapter with the definitions and statements; redo the examples
   by hand, with pen and paper, before looking at the solution.
2. Use the [interactive graphs](interactive/index.md) to get a feeling for what
   a theorem says or how a function changes.
3. Review the [exam theorems](exam-theorems.md): statement, hypotheses, proof.
