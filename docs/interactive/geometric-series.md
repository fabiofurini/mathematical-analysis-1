# Geometric series

The partial sums of \(\sum q^k\): change the ratio \(q\) and see when they converge, oscillate or diverge. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
