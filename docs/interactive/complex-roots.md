# Complex n-th roots

The \(n\) solutions of \(z^n = w\) in the complex plane: choose the index \(n\) and the modulus and argument of \(w\), and watch the vertices of the regular polygon. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
