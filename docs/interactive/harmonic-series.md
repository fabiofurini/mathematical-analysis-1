The series \(\sum 1/n^p\), with positive and with alternating terms: vary \(p\) and compare the partial sums. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
