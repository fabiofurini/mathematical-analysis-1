# Bisection method

At every step the interval is halved and still contains the zero: advance step by step and compare the error with the theoretical bound. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
