# Exponentials and logarithms

The exponential and the logarithm with base \(a\), symmetric about the line \(y = x\): move the base and see growth turn into decay. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
