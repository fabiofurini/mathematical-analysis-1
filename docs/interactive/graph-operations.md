# Operations on graphs

Translations, stretches and reflections of a graph: the four coefficients of \(a f(bx + c) + d\) move with the sliders. Drag the sliders, or press ▶ to animate. On the graph, pan by dragging with two fingers (or the mouse) and zoom with Shift + mouse wheel.
