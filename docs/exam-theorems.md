# Exam theorems

The list of theoretical results of the course that you must be able to
**state and prove**. Next to each one, the chapter of the notes where the proof
is given.

!!! tip "How to study them"
    For each theorem: read the statement and try to rebuild the proof on your
    own; only then open the «Proof» box in the chapter and compare.
